The private website database is the system of record. When configured, daily reports may be emailed to the sales team and approved fields synchronized to a private Google Sheet. Delivery is not guaranteed by submitting the form.

Requests are retained for follow-up under the deployment’s retention policy. Reports do not delete requests automatically. For access, correction or deletion requests, email sales1@evisionindia.com.

Cookies

Owner sign-in uses an essential session cookie. Rate limits use short-lived hashed network identifiers. No advertising trackers are included.

What not to send

Do not submit device credentials, passwords, identity documents, payment information or sensitive footage.
